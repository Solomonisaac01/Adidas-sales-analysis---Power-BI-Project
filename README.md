# Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard -- Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

**Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive sales analytics
project designed to explore Adidas sales performance across time
periods, U.S. states, regions, products, sales channels, and retailers.

The dashboard brings key performance indicators and visual summaries
together to help users understand sales, profit, units sold, average
price per unit, and profit margin.

This project is suitable for a data analytics portfolio and demonstrates
how a business dashboard can turn sales data into clear,
decision-supporting insights.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🎯 Business Problem

A retail business needs to understand where revenue is coming from,
which products and retailers perform best, and how sales change over
time and across channels.

This dashboard helps explore questions such as:

-   What are the overall sales, profit, units sold, average price per
    unit, and profit margin?
-   How do sales change from month to month?
-   Which U.S. states contribute to sales?
-   Which regions contribute the most to total sales?
-   Which products generate the highest sales?
-   Which retailers contribute the most to sales?
-   How can sales be explored by channel and year?

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard views, a business could:

1.  **Investigate high-performing months** such as July to understand
    whether promotions, seasonal demand, or other factors contributed to
    the increase.
2.  **Review lower-performing months** such as March to identify
    potential opportunities for campaigns or product offers.
3.  **Prioritize product analysis** for Men's Street Footwear while
    continuing to monitor the performance of other product groups.
4.  **Review retailer performance** and explore the practices, product
    mix, or channel strategy associated with higher sales.
5.  **Use geographic analysis** to investigate state and regional
    differences before making location-specific decisions.
6.  **Compare sales channels** using the In-store, Online, and Outlet
    filters to understand how channel performance varies.
7.  **Track profit alongside sales** so that decisions consider
    profitability as well as revenue.

## 🧹 Analysis Workflow

A typical workflow for a dashboard of this type can include:

1.  **Data preparation** -- review the sales data and check field
    formats and completeness.
2.  **Metric validation** -- confirm the definitions and calculations
    for sales, profit, units sold, average price per unit, and profit
    margin.
3.  **Data modeling** -- organize the fields needed for time, geography,
    product, retailer, and sales-channel analysis.
4.  **Visual analysis** -- compare monthly trends, regions, states,
    products, and retailers.
5.  **Dashboard development** -- arrange KPI cards, charts, maps, and
    interactive filters into a single report page.
